The supplied README states: "16 free components from the Annnimate library,

Local changes: retain the original photograph while the shader loads; render
before revealing the canvas; do not change global GSAP lag-smoothing settings.
The portfolio wrapper lazy-loads this effect, unmounts it when off screen,
and uses the original image when reduced motion is requested or WebGL fails.
